Burst every 60 s: [a:7390:Delirium] (→ [a:36928:Scarlet Delirium], [a:36929:Comeuppance], [a:36930:Torcleaver]), [a:3643:Carve and Spit], [a:3639:Salted Earth] + [a:25755:Salt and Darkness], [a:25757:Shadowbringer] charges; [a:16472:Living Shadow] (120 s) and [a:36932:Disesteem] open the two-minute window.

Checks xivanalysis applies to this job:
- This analyzer aims to help you unleash your inner edge. The core of Dark Knight offense focuses on maximizing blood and MP utilization, in raid buff windows where available. In addition, you should aim to land all three of the free attacks granted by [a:7390:Delirium]. Defensively, [a:7393:The Blackest Night] is a powerful cooldown that can mitigate heavy damage on you or a target, and grants a free use of Edge of Shadow or Flood of Shadow as long as the shield is fully consumed.
- Avoid letting your Blood Gauge overcap - the wasted resources may cost you uses of your spenders over the course of the fight.
- Blood Weapon: about 3 GCDs expected per window.
- Try to land 3 GCDs during every [a:3625:Blood Weapon] window.
- Darkside: xivanalysis target 99% uptime.
- Darkside is gained by using [a:16470:Edge of Shadow] or [a:16469:Flood of Shadow] and provides you with a 10% damage increase. As such, it is a significant part of a DRK's personal DPS. Do your best not to let it drop, and recover it as quickly as possible if it does.
- Each [a:7390:Delirium] window should contain [a:36928:Scarlet Delirium], [a:36929:Comeuppance], and [a:36930:Torcleaver], or three [a:36931:Impalement]s. Using non-Delirium combo actions resets your Delirium combo progress and causes you to lose the increased potency of the comboed skills.
- [a:16472:Living Shadow] lost potency due to either not executing its full rotation or being interrupted, such as by being out of range part way through. [a:16472:Living Shadow] accounts for a lot of your damage, make sure to use it when it will be able to execute its full rotation, and in a position where it will not become out of range.
- When the shield from [a:7393:The Blackest Night] is fully consumed you gain a Dark Arts proc, which allows free use of [a:16470:Edge of Shadow] or [a:16469:Flood of Shadow]. You should make sure each usage of [a:7393:The Blackest Night] will fully consume the shield, and that you use each Dark Arts proc before using [a:7393:The Blackest Night] again.
- Your MP allows you to use [a:16470:Edge of Shadow], a strong attack that gives you a persistent damage up buff, as well as the strong mitigation of [a:7393:The Blackest Night]. Be sure to consistently use your MP so you can benefit from natural regeneration and MP gain from your main combo skills.
- Use [a:25755:Salt and Darkness] every time you use [a:3639:Salted Earth] in order to do additional damage to enemies inside the [a:3639:Salted Earth] area of effect.
- Use [a:25755:Salt and Darkness] only when there are enemies inside the effect area of [a:3639:Salted Earth] and those enemies are not invulnerable.
- While you can use [a:7390:Delirium] either before or during your potion window, you should be sure to fit all three [a:7390:Delirium] enhanced skills within the potion window.
- Try to cover as much damage as possible with your Tinctures of Strength.
