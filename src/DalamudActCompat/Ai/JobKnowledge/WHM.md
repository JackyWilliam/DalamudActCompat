Role: healer. No party damage buff.
Burst: [a:136:Presence of Mind] (120 s) → three [a:37009:Glare IV] inside the party's two-minute buffs; [a:3571:Assize] every 40 s; [a:16535:Afflatus Misery] (from three lily heals) should land in buffs when possible.
Keep [s:1871:Dia] up without early refreshes; heal with lilies and oGCDs ([a:3570:Tetragrammaton], [a:7432:Divine Benison], [a:25861:Aquaveil], [a:3569:Asylum], [a:16536:Temperance]) before GCD heals: every Cure is a lost Glare.
Checks xivanalysis applies to this job:
- Try to use [a:37011:Divine Caress] whenever you have used [a:16536:Temperance]. It is a free, groupwide oGCD shield and heal over time!
- As a White Mage, [s:1871:Dia] is significant portion of your sustained damage. Aim to keep it up at all times.
- Avoid refreshing Dia significantly before its expiration, this will allow you to cast more Glares.
- Make sure to use all three [a:37009:Glare IV] procs after using [a:136:Presence of Mind]. It is your best offensive skill to use for the thirty second buff duration.
- Avoid interrupting casts by either prepositioning yourself or utilizing slidecasting where possible. Use windows created by normal [a:16532:Dia] refreshes to move in advance of mechanics. When that's not an option, try to plan and utilize Afflatus actions to simultaneously heal wherever needed and cover movement. Overwriting Dia early should be your last resort for movement.
- Try to use [a:16535:Afflatus Misery] to avoid wasting Blood Lily growth from overcapping the gauge.
- Try to use [a:16534:Afflatus Rapture] or [a:16531:Afflatus Solace] before using other GCD heals. It's okay to overheal with your lilies as they are to be used for mana management and movement aswell as healing.
- Try to finish the fight with no leftover Blood Lilies.
- Try to use Lilies instead of GCD heals when they're available.
- Cast a spell with [a:7561:Swiftcast] before it expires. This allows you to instantly cast spells with a cast times, such as [a:125:Raise] for a quick revive, or [a:25859:Glare III] for weaving.
- The main use of [a:7430:Thin Air] should be to save MP on high MP-cost spells. Don't be afraid to hold it and lose a use over the fight as long as it covers an MP-heavy spell such as usages of [a:37010:Medica III], [a:131:Cure III], and [a:125:Raise]. Usages that did not save a considerable amount of MP are marked red.
- Try to cover as much damage as possible with your Tinctures of Mind.
- Use [a:25862:Liturgy of the Bell] in an encounter when required to heal multiple hits in a row.
- Use [a:3569:Asylum] frequently in an encounter whenever party members will be close enough to stand in the bubble. For instances where members are frequently spread too far, Asylum can still be used for tank healing.
- Use [a:25861:Aquaveil] as often as possible to minimize single target healing needed.
- Weave [a:7432:Divine Benison] as often as possible to minimize single target healing needed. However, it is not worth clipping to apply a benison. Do not hold Divine Benison for tank busters. Since Dia's duration is 30s, try to weave Divine Benison with every Dia that you don't have two more important weaves to execute.
- Use [a:16536:Temperance] often to mitigate incoming raid damage and boost GCD healing potency. Avoid clipping to apply it.
