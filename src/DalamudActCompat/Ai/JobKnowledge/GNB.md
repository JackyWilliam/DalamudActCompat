Checks xivanalysis applies to this job:
- Avoid having leftover ammo at the end of a fight, consider using the ammo earlier if possible. [a:16162:Burst Strike] is more potency than any of your [a:16145:Solid Barrel] combo.
- Wasted cartridge generation, ending the fight with cartridges loaded, or dying with cartridges loaded is a direct potency loss. Use [a:16162:Burst Strike] (or [a:16163:Fated Circle] if there is more than one target) to avoid wasting cartridges.
- Avoid dropping your [a:16155:Continuation] procs. They are a significant portion of your damage and should be used as soon as possible.
- Gunbreaker can follow up cartridge skills with [a:16155:Continuation] skills. Make sure to usse them immediately as they will fall off if another GCD is pressed.
- Gunbreaker has a small dps optimization technique that can be done by using the extra charge of [a:16146:Gnashing Fang] from the half minute of your rotation in order to enter [a:16138:No Mercy] at the following times:
- If you are running at a slower GCD (2.47 or slower), you will only do 8 GCDs under [a:16138:No Mercy] so you will use [a:16138:No Mercy] right before a [a:16155:Continuation], ideally [a:16158:Eye Gouge].

- This technique is not always possible or correct in the event that [a:16138:No Mercy] is interrupted, and as such the [a:16138:No Mercy] module does not expect it, but this technique should be used whenever you can.
- Watch your uses of certain abilities during [a:16138:No Mercy]. Under ideal conditions, you should be using [a:16153:Sonic Break], a full [a:16146:Gnashing Fang] combo, and all of your off-GCD skills [a:16165:Blasting Zone], [a:16159:Bow Shock].
- Try to cover as much damage as possible with your Tinctures of Strength.
